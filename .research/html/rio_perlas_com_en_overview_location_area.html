Location & Area

Rio Perlas Hotel Resort & Spa is only a 50 kilometers drive (45 minutes) from San Jose airport. Our resort lays on a 110 hectares of rainforest in the beautiful and legendary Orosi Valley, in the province of Cartago. Visitors traveling to Costa Rica can escape the hectic hustle and bustle of daily life. Orosi Valley is home to some of the most delicious coffee in Costa Rica. The green valley has a humid climate, with a year-round temperature of about 24 degrees Celsius, or 75 degrees Fahrenheit
