Day Pass

Enjoy a stunning full day at Rio Perlas Spa & Resort! Experience the beauty of the rain forest, swim inside the rejuvenating hot thermal pool, dine in the divining restaurant with a beverage of your choice, watch the unique view of the water falls, and look up to see the rare birds nesting & flying around the Resort. Don’t miss on this opportunity, dreams can be real, see you there

Information about the day pass:

Prices:

Over the age of 18 - 35 USD

Under the age of 18 - 20 USD

Time: Every day between 9am – 9pm

The daily entrance includes: entrance to the resort complex, entrance to the swimming/thermal pools, lunch, and use of the resort facilities (at an additional charge - an individual fee per facility)

 

The lunch includes:

One appetizer, one Main course, one dessert, one squeezed juice

Appetizer menu:

Caesar salad, vegetable salad, pumpkin soup, tomato salad

Main course Main:

Meat dish with mushrooms,

Fried meat

Chicken breast with taffo sauce

Fried chicken breast
